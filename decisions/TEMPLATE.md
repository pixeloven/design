# DDR-NNNN: The decision, as a short statement

**Status:** proposed · **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What needed deciding, and the evidence in front of the decision. Link the
report, study or review page rather than restating it.

## Decision

The decision itself, in one or two sentences. When the operator decided it,
quote their words exactly and say when they said them.

## Consequences

What changes because of it: entries to add or retire, packages to bump,
consumers to move, checks that now hold. Name what it does not decide.

## Sources

- Each place this decision is written or was argued, as a link.
