# DDR-0002: Cool/Warm themes, Acid/Violet accents and appearance are independent choices

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-29, first written in the foundations plan.
**Backfilled** on 2026-11-21. This record adds no new content.

## Decision

> The operator approved Solid as the primary figure, Inset for icons, Cool/Warm
> themes, and Acid/electric Violet accents.
>
> — [`docs/brand-foundations-plan.md`](../docs/brand-foundations-plan.md#approved-brand-foundations)

> Cool and Warm are shared themes. Acid and electric Violet are independent
> accent choices. Dark, Light and System appearance remain independent of both.
>
> — [`docs/brand-family.md`](../docs/brand-family.md#established-decisions), Established decisions

The plan's acceptance adds: "New theme and accent choices are typed and
independent of appearance."
