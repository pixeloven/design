# Warden Branch mark

A candidate identity for Warden that was not selected: two lower paths converge
into one upward bearing under a large arrowhead. Direction reads immediately,
and the split tail keeps the idea of coordinated work.

**Do not use in production.** Warden's identity is Waymark, in `@pixeloven/brand`.

## Dates

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Drawn | October 2026, for the [Warden identity study](../studies/warden-identity/README.md); first committed 2026-10-23 |
| Not selected | 2026-10-25, when the operator selected Waymark |
| Retired | Brand 0.3.0 (`brand-v0.3.0`, 2026-10-25), which published Waymark |

## What it was

A Solid figure and an Inset form cut into the family container, both on a
64×64 viewBox in neutral `currentColor`, with no palette of its own. At 16px the
Inset kept its arrow and fork; the two tail tips became secondary.

## Why it was retired

- It had the strongest directional reading of the three and depended least on
  a letter, but it can resemble a launch or upload symbol, so it was less
  distinctive for the product than Waymark.
- The October 25 selection review found that it reads as upload or launch
  before it reads as coordinated work.

## What replaced it

**Waymark**, the angular W: selected on 2026-10-25, published in brand 0.3.0
and adopted by Warden ([DDR-0004](../../decisions/0004-warden-waymark.md)).
