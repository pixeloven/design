## Platform

web

## Users

The PixelOven operator reviews product direction on desktop and phone.
Developers and coding agents use the documentation to consume shared packages
consistently. These roles and the cross-product reuse goal were established in
the original brand-review discussion and carried forward for this closeout.

## Product Purpose

Show what the design system actually ships and provide concrete, interactive
material for reviewing product design. Published references and experimental
studies must be distinguishable. Success is choosing the right asset or control,
understanding its contract, and evaluating a proposed task without mistaking it
for a live application.

## Operating Context

Storybook is the design-system reference. Portable previews support direct-file
and trusted-network review when a session link cannot render an HTML file.
Harmony Homepage should link to the verified current documentation after the
brand/design closeout; that consumer update is the final phase, not part of
building a study.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing React/Vite/Storybook application reads canonical token and brand
sources and consumes shared native controls. Warden studies contain synthetic
work with local state only; they perform no API, MCP or vault writes.

Warden serves humans turning ideas into actionable work; agents use MCP.
It retains six fixed workflow states. Source capabilities and the accepted
interaction improvements are detailed in
`../../docs/warden-design-brief.md`. The operator supported the October study's
visual direction but rejected its queue-centered project/task model. The target
is now ordinary issue capture and progressive refinement. Everything is an
Issue; Epic is a bounded project outcome with a finish line, alongside Story,
Task, Bug and Spike types. Standalone work remains valid. Type is separate from
workflow status. Spikes record learning that can refine existing requirements
or yield linked follow-up work; there is no required idea intake or Epic.
New issue offers all five types in one composer. New Epic preselects that type
in the same flow; a title alone is sufficient and type-specific fields adapt.
The study demonstrates creation, investigation, refinement, organization,
direction and outcome acceptance using browser-local
records. The production UI/MCP MVP and full owned-stack ingestion/restart
acceptance merged in Warden #80 on October 25. The versioned handoff names the
validated source and separate customer cutover work; this synthetic study does
not establish a deployed capability. Mark selection and shared-control adoption
remain part of design closeout.
The subsequent operator review identified unclear attention requests, scattered
task actions and missing drag feedback. The revised interaction puts the exact
ask and actions together, defaults Activity to Comments, makes project counts actionable
and previews a drag's destination and insertion before committing it.
Responsibility and the required next actor are authored sample fields, not live
execution telemetry. Existing API gaps are delivery work, not limits on the
proposed experience. Agent dispatch and automatic structured history are not
established capabilities.

## Brand Commitments

PixelOven is the parent; Lattice and Warden are products. Carry forward the
operator-approved modern, minimalist cyberpunk direction, Cool/Warm themes,
Acid/electric Violet accents, readable Plex typography, and Solid/Inset identity
roles. PixelOven and Lattice figures are approved; Warden's figure remains a
review decision. These commitments constrain the study rather than initiating
a new visual-world selection.

## Evidence on Hand

Published token, brand and UI package sources; accepted Lattice implementation;
October 19 independent Warden live/source reviews; recovered September
project-first prototype, product direction and journeys. The Warden brief
reconciles them with Lattice's later visual and interaction lessons. Historical
demos remain references. Private live captures and command logs stay outside
Git. Review content is authored synthetic material and carries no real delivery
or test-result claims.

## Product Principles

1. Show behavior and consequences through usable examples.
2. Keep one source for shared values and production assets.
3. Separate a proposed experience from a shipped capability.
4. Preserve the person's task when appearance or presentation changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Usability and accessibility are first-class project requirements. Review phone
and desktop layouts, keyboard behavior, enlarged text, theme pairings and
failure states. Automated checks supplement named device and assistive-technology
checks; they do not establish unperformed coverage.
